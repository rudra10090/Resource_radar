Why this matters

Shared spaces like hostels, college campuses, and co-working offices often waste staggering amounts of energy and water while generating excess landfill waste — simply because no single person feels personally accountable or has an easy way to alert facilities teams.

Energy impact

Empty hostel common rooms and unused computer labs frequently leave lighting and climate control running non-stop.

Water loss

Leaking taps in shared bathrooms often go unreported for days, leaking upwards of 3,000 liters per fixture each month.

Waste sorting

Mixed disposal bins in cafeterias cause over 60% of otherwise clean recyclable packaging to end up in local municipal dumps.
