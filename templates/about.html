Our mission

Why we built this

Shared spaces like hostels, campuses, offices, and apartment societies waste large amounts of energy, water, and generate excess waste — because no single person feels personally accountable the way they would in their own home. When resource consumption is decentralized and utility costs are absorbed collectively, everyday waste goes unnoticed. ResourceRadar exists to give these spaces transparent visibility and a simple, frictionless way to report and track inefficiencies.

The challenge

The problem with shared spaces

In communal environments, individual accountability is inherently low when resources are shared and bills aren't personal. Without direct feedback loops or designated ownership, minor leaks and unattended utilities become normalized habits.

Energy

Lights and fans left running in empty rooms.

Water

Leaking taps in shared bathrooms going unreported for days.

Waste

Excess food waste in canteens with no recycling in place.

Workflow

How it works

A simple three-step cycle that connects community awareness with immediate operational resolution.

Step 01

Report

Spot an inefficiency? Report it in seconds with location and category.

Step 02

Track

Reports are visible to the community, so nothing gets forgotten.

Step 03

Improve

Authorities and residents act on visible, tracked issues.

Small changes in shared spaces add up to real impact.
