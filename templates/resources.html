Resources

Practical tips to reduce resource wastage in your shared space.

💡 Did You Know?

Set AC to 24°C or above — every degree lower increases energy consumption by 6%.

💡

Turn Off Lights

Turn off lights when leaving a room — even for 5 minutes saves electricity.

🌀

Fans Over AC

Use fans instead of AC whenever possible — fans use 10x less energy.

🔌

Unplug Chargers

Chargers consume phantom power even when idle. Unplug when not in use.

☀️

Use Natural Light

Open curtains during the day instead of switching on lights.

🌡️

Set AC to 24°C

Every degree below 24°C increases energy use by 6% — set it wisely.

🔧

Report Broken Switches

Broken switches waste energy constantly — report them immediately.

💡

Use LED Bulbs

LED bulbs use 75% less energy than regular bulbs — switch where possible.

🌙

Switch Off Common Area Lights

Switch off corridor and staircase lights during daytime.

🔋

Power Strips

Use power strips with switches to cut off multiple devices at once.

🏢

Report Empty Room AC

Report AC units running in empty rooms or common areas.

🚿

Shorter Showers

Every minute saved in the shower = 8-10 litres of water saved.

🔧

Report Leaking Taps

A dripping tap wastes up to 20,000 litres per year — report it immediately.

🪣

Use Buckets

Use buckets instead of running taps when washing clothes by hand.

🚰

Turn Off Taps

Turn off taps while brushing teeth — saves up to 6 litres per minute.

🏠

Report Overflow

Overflowing water tanks on rooftops waste hundreds of litres daily.

🌿

Water Plants Smart

Water plants in the evening to reduce evaporation loss.

🔍

Report Seepage

Wet patches on walls or floors indicate hidden leaks — report them.

🚿

Avoid Running Taps

Avoid leaving taps running while washing utensils in shared kitchens.

💧

Use Only What You Need

Fill buckets or containers with only as much water as needed.

📢

Encourage Awareness

Remind hostel floor-mates to be mindful of water use during peak hours.

🗂️

Separate Waste

Separate wet waste (food) and dry waste (paper, plastic) in your room.

♻️

Recycle

Use designated recycling bins for paper, plastic, and metal waste.

🧴

No Single-Use Plastic

Carry a reusable water bottle and bags to avoid single-use plastic.

🍱

Reduce Food Waste

Order only what you'll finish — food waste in canteens is a major issue.

📱

E-Waste Disposal

Dispose of old chargers and batteries at designated e-waste collection points.

📄

Save Paper

Use both sides of paper and avoid printing unless absolutely necessary.

👕

Donate Old Items

Donate old clothes, books, and stationery instead of throwing them away.

🌱

Compost

Compost fruit and vegetable waste if your campus has a compost facility.

📢

Report Overflowing Bins

Report overflowing dustbins in common areas so they get cleared promptly.

🚫

No Burning Waste

Avoid burning waste — it is harmful and often illegal in residential areas.
